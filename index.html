Sunday · An adda under the mountain

Welcome picnic

An adda is a Bengali tradition, essentially the art of hanging out. Think long, easy, unstructured conversation with good people, unhurried chats that go wherever they go.

That's exactly the spirit we want to start the week with. Join us for a mountain-side picnic, meet each other, swap some stories, play some games and stay for as long as you like.

We'll hand out your Holud outfit here, so do come to this one if you can.

What actually happens

The holud is a traditional ceremony that dates back to Affy's forefathers' times, where marriages were typically arranged and the bride and groom would be meeting for the first time.

The traditions include “looking through the mirror” which involves the bride and groom seeing each other for the first time - but looking through the gaze of a mirror.

There will also be skits involved - this will be fun. Where Gabby's friends will act out her past so we can get a sneak peek into who Affy's really getting tied up with. And vice versa where Affy's mates will no doubt do a massive disservice to his spotless past.

We're providing traditional Bengali wear for everyone: kurtas and saris. Accessories should be gold and bright. We'll hand the outfits out at the welcome picnic on the 14th, so the RSVP asks for a size per person.

Saturday · A wild dream at the edge of the world

The wedding

When
Sat 20 March 2027, from 15h00
Where
Buffelsdrift Game Lodge, Oudtshoorn
Dress
Earthy and natural. See what to wear

Following the beautiful Route 62, there will be coaches picking us up from Cape Town, and all guests will travel about five hours into the wilderness of the Karoo. The coaches arrive the night before the wedding, with accommodation scattered around the venue and in the quaint little town of Oudtshoorn.

The Karoo is a sight to see. During the day you'll spot majestic giraffes, zebras and South African wildlife, and you'll have the opportunity to go on a local game drive at Buffelsdrift too.

On the day of the wedding, those staying out of town get coached in around midday. We'll have a toast, speeches, and one last celebration to tie out the adventure, dancing into the night under the stars in the vast expanse of the Karoo. A place like no other.
