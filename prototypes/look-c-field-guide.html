I  ·  An adda under the mountain

Welcome picnic

When
Sun 14 March, 15h00 – 19h00
Where
Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens, Cape Town
Dress
Picnic florals

An adda is a Bengali tradition, essentially the art of hanging out. Think long, easy, unstructured conversation with good people, unhurried chats that go wherever they go.

We'll hand out your Holud outfit here, so do come to this one if you can.

II  ·  Gilded and ungovernable

Holud

When
Tue 16 March, 15h00 – 21h00
Where
Norval Foundation gardens, Cape Town
Dress
We're providing your outfit

People come to the holud (which actually means yellow but also means turmeric) wearing really colourful clothes. If you're boring and bland don't worry we've got you covered for this fun event only.
